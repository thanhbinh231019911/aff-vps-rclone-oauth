AFF VPS Backup Rclone

This is a personal backup connector used by the owner of the AFF VPS project to copy backup archives from a private VPS to the owner's Google Drive using rclone.

The app is not offered as a public service and is not used for advertising, analytics, resale, or sharing of Google user data.

Google Drive access

Google Drive access is used only for owner-initiated backup, verification, restore, and related file-management operations for the owner's own backup data.
