Privacy Policy — AFF VPS Backup Rclone

Last updated: 2026-10-05.

Purpose

AFF VPS Backup Rclone is a personal-use connector that uses rclone to back up and restore the owner's private VPS data to and from the owner's Google Drive.

Google user data accessed

The app may access Google Drive file and folder metadata and file contents as needed to perform backup, verification, restore, listing, copy, upload, download, and deletion operations initiated by the owner or the owner's scheduled backup system.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore functionality described above. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, sale, or marketing.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing. The software used for file transfer is rclone; operations are performed on infrastructure controlled by the owner.

Credential storage

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored on the owner's VPS with restricted operating-system permissions. They are not published on this website and are not stored in this repository.

Retention and deletion

Backup files are retained according to the owner's backup retention policy. The owner can delete backup files from Google Drive and can revoke this app's Google account access at any time from Google account security settings.

Contact

This app is for the owner's personal use. For privacy questions, use the support/contact email shown on the app's Google OAuth consent screen.
